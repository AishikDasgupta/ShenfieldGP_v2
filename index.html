Book Mole Mapping

Your mole mapping visit, step by step

Consultation with Dr K

Your appointment begins with a personal consultation — Dr K conducts the entire mapping process himself. A chaperone is available, and a family member is welcome to attend.

Automated total body photography

An automatic lift positions the camera to your height and body shape, capturing four rapid photos of each side of your body.

Digital stitching & dermoscopy

Photos are stitched into a comprehensive whole-body view with detailed pictures of each mole. Any mole of concern is captured in extra detail with microimaging.

Same-visit review & report

Dr K reviews the images with you — confirming no moles of concern, or identifying any that need review, with a report for NHS or private referral.

Personalised follow-up plan

Based on your risk, we recommend when to return — 3–6 months or annually. Repeat visits highlight new or changed moles, essential for early detection.
